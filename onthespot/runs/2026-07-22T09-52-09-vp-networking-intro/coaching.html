the single fix for this session

Lead every substantive turn with a one-sentence answer to the exact question, then stop and let the counterpart pull the next thread.
The whole conversation stalled because the answer to 'what is it' never came first; the VP had to dig five times and left saying he would have to try it to get it. Answer-first would have converted the hook he earned into interest instead of losing it.
drill Next session, before each reply, say the single sentence that answers the literal question out loud first, even if it feels blunt, then add context only if asked. Cap the opening at one sentence.
