## spoken_register
A uses contractions, short flat lines, and real speech rhythm: "Right, you're paying a margin either way," and the clipped "Okay, noted." B's sentences are also longer and more written ("Optionality is a nice talking point until you are debugging why..."). A sounds spoken; B sounds composed. Winner: A.

## variety
A changes shape: probes in turns 1 to 4, a flat refuse-to-rule in turn 5, and a pure assertion in turn 6 with no question at all. B runs the identical move six times: name the true part, pivot with "but", close with "Have you actually...". The practicer could set a watch by B's cadence. Winner: A.

## practice_value

A, at the same beat, eases off with "you've got the right question," half-validating the dodge, and it also softens turn 1 with "you own the stack." B never flatters and keeps the evidence screw turning. A applies real pressure elsewhere (the cycles-per-quarter demand, the supplier-stays-dumb bet) but whiffs the one moment that most needed a hard press. Winner: B.
