# pmcurve Advanced Growth PM course

Course notes from pmcurve's Advanced Growth PM program (pmcurve.com): growing consumer companies through growth models, retention, engagement, activation, acquisition, and pricing. The running case is Natura, a fictional marketplace; the course's own dataset lives at query.pmcurve.com (referenced in module 06, line 10087). Paid course content, absorbed for Ben's personal use only.

- Quantitative growth model and projections (why "10% MoM sounds fine" targets fail, output vs input metrics, Day-N retention in the DAU breakdown): 06:2980, monthly customer formula at 06:3194, diagnosing slowdown at 06:3285.
