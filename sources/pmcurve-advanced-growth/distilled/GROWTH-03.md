**Competency:** Product Growth
**Coverage:** Strong

## Definition
Optimizing the user journey from signup to first value realization, including onboarding design, time-to-value reduction, and aha moment engineering.

## Key Frameworks

### Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAT)
**Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Trigger**

All three must be present for action to occur.

### Motivators (FBM)
| Motivator | Positive | Negative |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Sensation | Pleasure | Pain |
| Anticipation | Hope | Fear |
| Belonging | Acceptance | Rejection |

**Key Insight:** Motivation is fickle and hard to sustain. Don't rely on it continuously.

### Ability Factors
1. **Time:** Opportunity cost consideration
2. **Money:** Budget constraints
3. **Physical Effort:** Body preserves calories
4. **Mental Effort:** People prefer autopilot
5. **Non-routine:** Deviation from habits requires effort

### Trigger Types
| Trigger | Target User | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Spark** | High ability, low motivation | Inspirational quote notification |
| **Facilitator** | High motivation, low ability | 50% discount notification |
| **Signal** | High both, unaware | New stock available notification |

### Product Levers for B=MAT
**Motivation Levers:**
- Social proof (ratings, reviews)
- Progress bars
- Scarcity messaging
- Authority citations
- Identity/badges

**Ability Levers:**
- Guiding users (tooltips, hotspots, overlays)
- Customization for segments
- Improved search/recommendations

### Forcefulness Spectrum
| Low Force | Medium Force | High Force |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Tooltip | Hotspot | Blur overlay |
| Skip option | Prompted selection | Required action |

**Decision factors:** Certainty of recommendation, criticality to experience, number of options

## Proof of Work

### Doing It (Work Context)
- Designing onboarding flows using Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAT: Motivation × Ability × Trigger)
- Selecting appropriate trigger types: Spark (low motivation), Facilitator (low ability), Signal (unaware)
- Optimizing forcefulness spectrum based on certainty, criticality, and number of options

### Demonstrating It (Interview/Review Context)
- Product sense interviews often involve activation — show you understand B=MAT framework
- Key principle: "Motivation is fickle — reduce ability requirements instead of trying to increase motivation"
- Reference trigger selection: match trigger type to the user's state (motivation vs ability gap)
- *Practice: any product sense problem where you design the first-time user experience*
