## Key Frameworks

1. **The Bump Offer Strategy**
   - Stage 1: Acquire customer (accept CAC)
   - Stage 2: Front-end sale (break even)
   - Stage 3: One-click upsell (profit)
   - Stage 4: Order bump at checkout (additional profit)
   - Key: Each stage increases customer value without acquisition cost

2. **Monetization Ladder**
   - Service (high touch, learn the problem)
   - Productized Service (standardized delivery)
   - SaaS Product (automated, scalable)
   - Portfolio (multiple products, shared infrastructure)

3. **Pricing Validation**
   - Ask: "Does this price point make sense for this target avatar?"
   - Test before manufacturing
   - Validate dropship → then produce inventory

4. **Revenue Metrics**
   - Target Conversion: 3-5%
   - Target CAC: < Product Price
   - LTV should justify CAC within acceptable payback period
