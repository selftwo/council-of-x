## Key Frameworks

### PM Role in Acquisition Channels
| Channel | PM Role | Key Activities |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Paid Marketing | Assist | Personalization, landing pages, tracking |
| Product Referrals | Lead | Incentive design, UX, value proposition |
| SEO/Content | Lead | Technical SEO, UGC mechanisms |
| Tools/Freemium | Lead | Free tool creation, conversion flows |

### Channel Analysis Framework
1. **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Spend / Customers Acquired
2. **Gross Profit:** Revenue - COGS per customer
3. **Payback Period:** CAC / Monthly Gross Profit
4. **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Target > 3:1

### Paid Marketing PM Contributions
- **Tracking & Attribution:** Event tracking, channel attribution
- **Retargeting Personalization:** Show relevant items, not just viewed items
- **Landing Page Optimization:** Copy, conversion, engagement
- **Localization:** Vernacular ads for expanded market

### SEO & Content Strategy
- **Technical SEO:** Site structure, crawlability, performance
- **UGC Mechanisms:** Reviews, ratings, Q&A
- **AI Content Tools:** 10x faster content creation at lower cost

### Attribution Models
| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| First Click | 100% to first touch | Brand awareness campaigns |
| Last Click | 100% to final touch | E-commerce, repeat purchase |
| Linear | Equal across touches | Balanced analysis |
| Time Decay | Weighted toward recent | Long sales cycles |
| Position Based | First/last weighted | Multi-touch journeys |

## Proof of Work

### Doing It (Work Context)
- Analyzing acquisition channels by CAC, payback period, and LTV (not just CAC alone)
- Ensuring channel-model fit — matching pricing to viable distribution channels
- Collaborating with marketing on attribution, landing pages, and retargeting personalization

### Demonstrating It (Interview/Review Context)
- Growth interviews test channel strategy — show you understand channel economics beyond just CAC
- Key trap: the "dead zone" — pricing too high for self-serve, too low for sales
- Reference channel analysis: "Same CAC channels can have very different payback periods and LTV"
