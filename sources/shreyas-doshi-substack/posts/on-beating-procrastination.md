# On Beating Procrastination

*Procrastination has been a constant fixture of my life. Here's a short post on what's worked for me, after a lot of experimentation. See if it helps with your experimentation.*

If you find yourself procrastinating on something important, it sometimes helps to:

a) recognize that it is happening because of some latent fear,

b) then forgive yourself for it,

c) remind yourself that fear is a normal feature of life (for 99.999% of people in the world),

d) try to get to the root of the fear in this instance,

e) then, instead of wishing that fear away, tell yourself that you can still take the first step even though this fear exists i.e. create the Google Doc, start composing the email, send an invite for the contentious meeting, etc,

f) then, congratulate yourself for taking this first step, perhaps even rewarding yourself with something you love (even if it is a disproportionately high reward vs. the time/effort you put in),

g) then, either continuing with the momentum of that task, or switch to another more comfortable task but with the feeling of strength from having taken the first step in the face of the fear,

Next time, repeat this. Over time, it will get slightly easier. The fear never goes away (definitely hasn’t for me). But you do make more progress with practice.
