# Do you know what you are *really* selling?

*If you're a founder/CEO or a senior leader in a high leverage role, it is smart to identify what you are really selling.*

You can simplify complex decisions, if you understand what you are *really* selling.

Apple sells taste

Amazon sells convenience

Google sells utility

Disney sells nostalgia

Stripe sells deep care

Anthropic sells assistance

OpenAI sells answers

Meta sells connection, vanity, voyeurism

X / Twitter sells access

LinkedIn sells opportunity

Starbucks sells consistency

Costco sells curation

Visa sells trust

Amex sells prestige

Shopify sells independence

~
