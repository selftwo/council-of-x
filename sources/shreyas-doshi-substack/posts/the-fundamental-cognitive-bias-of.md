# The Fundamental Cognitive Bias of Product Builders

The fundamental cognitive bias of product builders has 4 embedded beliefs together:

  1. “My product is great, but it isn’t successful because I have a marketing & distribution problem”



  2. “My competitor’s product is successful mainly because of their marketing & distribution”

  3. “My product is better than my competitor’s”

  4. “To succeed, I must just keep making my product better”




Nearly every passionate product builder has this compound bias.

Some spot it eventually, others go through their entire career without ever spotting it.

A prerequisite to go from being a competent product builder to being an elite product builder is to:

\- see this bias in yourself [0]

\- understand how each individual statement is greatly flawed [1]

\- spot the basic logic error across these statements [2]

~

[0] In practice, this is very hard for passionate mid-career product builders because it requires us to admit how much we still need to learn about making successful products.

[1] Usually this understanding only comes with hard-won experience. But not everyone with experience reaches this understanding.

[2] [Check out this Claude chat](https://claude.ai/share/2cc2a252-0ece-4675-a4c7-de99a10872d9) for a comprehensive understanding of the logic error and the epistemic errors underlying this bias. It is a short read (~5 minutes), and for _some_ product builders at the _right phase in their career_ , it will be life changing.
