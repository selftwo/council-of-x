# Route Around Big Egos

*When two Type-A PMs negotiate, everyone loses.*

Product Managers in some top tier companies have a big ego, **along with high competitiveness / combativeness specifically against other PMs in the company1**.

So if you are trying to convince another team to do something, a nice trick for you as a PM is to **get the negotiation to happen via the EMs / TLs (or at times the Designers) of the 2 teams rather than leading the negotiation yourself with your Type-A combative PM counterpart on the other team**. 

This might be non-standard at your company, and it will require you to **operate more behind-the-scenes** than might be the standard way of doing inter-team work at your company, but **it will yield better outcomes than the standard approach of 2 Type-A PMs butting heads**.

What prevents some PMs from practicing this technique, even though the most talented PMs already know it works, is that they don’t care about product / team outcomes as much as they profess to. They do care about outcomes, of course, **but they care a lot more about something else** , something that’s more ego-centric than business outcomes.

This is an uncomfortable truth. And those who find it unbearably uncomfortable will instinctively manufacture a dozen clever objections against this truth. Which brings me to my final observation here:

**The smarter the person, the better the excuse.**

-Shreyas
