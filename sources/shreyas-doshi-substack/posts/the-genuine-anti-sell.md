# The Genuine Anti-Sell

Something counterintuitive I learned late in my career (by observing a manager of mine at Stripe):

If you are very competent, the **Genuine Anti-Sell** works like magic.

Example: when interviewing for a senior leadership role, be clear and transparent about reasons why you are _not_ a good fit.

This has two main effects:

  1. A competent hiring manager will be pleasantly surprised by your candor and awareness. You will stand out versus other candidates who are “selling.”

  2. If the hiring manager thinks you are good, they will respond with why they believe you’re a great fit. **Those beliefs will now get stronger.**




Last few thoughts:

a) This works in many situations that require influence (e.g., when you are trying to hire someone, want to convince a group, or want to sell your product).

b) This technique will work for very few people (probably <10% of people).12 It can easily backfire if used by others.

c) You must be genuine. This is not a “hack.” This is not a manipulation technique. You must actually believe the observations you are making as part of your anti-sell. If you try to use it as a hack, it will eventually backfire in bad ways.
