# Prioritization, Problem Solving, and Practice

**Session 2.4**

In this video, **Deepak** covers the crucial skills of prioritization and problem-solving, along with structuring practice for PM interviews.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. The Need for Prioritization
*   **Justification**: Prioritization is essential in interviews to demonstrate competence.
*   **End-to-End**: Showing the ability to solve a segment's problems **end-to-end (one path)** is more effective than shallowly covering multiple scenarios.

### 2. Critical Problem Solving
*   **MECE Tree**: Break down user challenges (e.g., low digital fluency) into a **Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive (MECE)** tree.
*   **Focus**: Prioritize by **eliminating non-focus areas** effectively.

### 3. Creative Problem Solving
*   **Method**: Involves **merging unrelated concepts** (by association).
*   **Goal**: This approach often leads to innovative **"10x thinking" solutions** that critical thinking alone cannot achieve.

### 4. Solution Prioritization (CRiCE Framework)
*   Introduced the **CRiCE framework** for prioritizing solutions:
    *   **R**each
    *   **I**mpact
    *   **C**onfidence
    *   **E**ffort
    *   **Strategic** alignment
*   **Time Constraints**: Note that for time-constrained scenarios, focusing just on **Effort and Impact** is often sufficient.

### 5. Weekly Practice Structure
*   **Routine**: Outline a structure of **daily problem-solving** and **mock interviews**.
*   **Objective**: Build articulation skills and reduce the **"processing load"** on the brain during an actual interview.
