# Analytical Interviews: North Star Metrics & Problem Types

**Analytical & Growth Module**

In this video, **Deepak** introduces the Analytical Interviews section, focusing on the core concept of North Star Metrics and the types of analytical problems encountered.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Skill vs. Interview-Ready Skill
*   **Insight**: Shared a personal story highlighting the difference between having a skill and being **interview-ready**.
*   **Practice**: Emphasized that rigorous practice is crucial, even for areas where you excel (like data analysis).

### 2. Five Analytical Problem Types
Categorized the analytical interviews into five shapes:
1.  **Defining Goals/Metrics**
2.  **RCA (Root Cause Analysis)**
3.  **Data-Driven Decision Making**
4.  **Guesstimates**
5.  **Market Sizing**

### 3. The North Star Metric (NSM)
*   **Purpose**: To **align all teams (vectors)** toward a single direction to maximize collective impact.
*   **Value**: Represents value for **both users and the business**.

### 4. Seven Criteria for a Good NSM
A good North Star Metric must be:
1.  A **direct indicator** of user value.
2.  A **leading indicator** of business value.
3.  **Accurately measurable**.
4.  **Hard to game** (avoid composite/ratio metrics).
*(And 3 others implied in the full framework)*

### 5. Pitfalls & Watchouts
*   **Composite Metrics**: Warned against metrics like conversion rate which can be misleading.
*   **Gameable Metrics**: Metrics like "time spent" can be gamed to the detriment of user experience.
*   **Ambiguity**: Ambiguous metrics lead to unintended consequences and goal misalignment.

### 6. Counter Metrics vs. Guardrails
*   **Counter Metrics**: Track **unintended consequences** (e.g., Revenue vs. Watch Time).
*   **Guardrails**: Define **non-negotiable system boundaries** (e.g., Maximum daily usage limits, page load latency).
