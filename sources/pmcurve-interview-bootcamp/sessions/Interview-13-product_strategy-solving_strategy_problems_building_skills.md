# Solving Strategy Problems and Building PM Skills

**Product Strategy Module**

In this video, **Deepak** outlines the next steps for the bootcamp and demonstrates the practical application of strategy using a real-world example.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Bootcamp Next Steps
*   **Mock Interviews**: Opening Calendly slots for Product Sense mock interviews (8-9 AM and 4-6 PM).
*   **Practice**: emphasized continuing **daily problem-solving** practice.

### 2. Practical Strategy Example: Reliance & Quick Commerce
*   **Case**: Tackling a large-scale strategy problem: **Reliance entering Quick Commerce**.
*   **Process**: Demonstrated the back-and-forth, **non-linear thinking** process required to solve such problems effectively.

### 3. Integrated Decision Making
*   **"Right to Win"**: Showcased how to align Reliance's strengths (existing stores, brand power, supplier leverage) to exploit market gaps.
*   **Focus**: Prioritizing **price and selection** as key vectors for disruption.

### 4. Judging Strategic Prowess
*   **Criteria**: In a job, strategic prowess is judged by the **coherence and well-roundedness** of ideas.
*   **Insight**: The ability to **uncover insights** and successfully align organizational vectors is paramount.

### 5. Core Goal
*   **Objective**: Generally, the bootcamp's goal is to cultivate **"world-class PM" skills**.
*   **Impact**: Focusing on fundamental product sense and strategic thinking inherently improves interview performance.
