# Problem Statement #1 (Strategy): Should Google enter the food industry?

**Product Strategy Practice**

This resource covers the first practice problem statement in the Product Strategy module.

## Problem Statement

**Should Google enter into the food industry?**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your strategy.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   **Strategic Lenses**: Right Industry? Right Time? Right to Win?
    *   **Fit**: How does this align with Google's core mission (organizing information) and capabilities?
    *   **Approach**: Buy, Build, or Partner?
    *   **Why/Why Not**: Formulate a strong argument backed by frameworks (e.g., Porter's Five Forces, 7 Powers).
