# Problem Statement #1 (Analytical): Measuring Traffic Congestion

**Analytical Practice**

This resource covers the first practice problem statement in the Analytical & Growth module, focusing on Metrics and Guesstimates.

## Problem Statement

**As a Google Maps PM, how will you measure traffic congestion in Bangalore?**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your approach.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   **Goal**: Define "Congestion" clearly (Speed vs. Volume vs. Delay).
    *   **Metrics**: Propose a North Star Metric (e.g., Average Delay per Mile) and supporting metrics.
    *   **Data Sources**: GPS data, User Reports, Historical patterns.
    *   **Trade-offs**: Accuracy vs. Real-time availability?
