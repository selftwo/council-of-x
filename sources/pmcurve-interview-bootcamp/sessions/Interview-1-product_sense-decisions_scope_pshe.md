# Product Sense: Decisions, Scope, and the PSHE Framework

**Session 2.1**

In this video, **Deepak** covers the foundational aspects of Product Sense in PM interviews, focusing on decision-making, scope, and the PSHE framework.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Setting the Context
*   The small cohort size is designed to allow for more **one-on-one interactions** and **mock interviews**.
*   This structure minimizes the need for extensive group introductions, focusing time on learning.

### 2. The Value of Live Attendance
*   **Strategy**: Removing AI note-takers to encourage **live attendance**.
*   **Rationale**: Live engagement ensures **transformation** and **discipline**, offering a deeper learning experience compared to passive consumption of videos or text.

### 3. Defining Product Sense
At its core, Product Sense is about **making the right decisions while dealing with ambiguity**.
It requires a combination of:
*   **User Understanding**
*   **Domain Understanding** (to be covered in the next session)
*   **Creative Problem-Solving**

### 4. The PSHE Framework
The **PSHE (Problem, Solution, How, Execution)** framework is introduced as a tool to understand the **scope of problems**.
*   It helps explain how the expected level of detail (P/S/H/E) changes across different **seniority levels** (PM, GPM, VP).

### 5. Problem Indexing
Demonstrated how to **predict the seniority level** of a product interview question by analyzing its scope.
*   *Example 1*: "Design an alarm clock for blind people" (More specific scope)
*   *Example 2*: "What should Google build next?" (Broad, ambiguous scope)
