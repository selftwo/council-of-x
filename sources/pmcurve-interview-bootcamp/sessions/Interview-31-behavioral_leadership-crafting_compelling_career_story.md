# Crafting a Compelling Career Story

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** dives deeper into the mechanics of constructing a powerful career narrative, emphasizing self-reflection, true leadership definition, and strategic career planning.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Self-Reflection & Mistakes
*   **Process**: Encouraged ongoing self-reflection to "prevent ignorance" and avoid repeating mistakes.
*   **Narrative**: Use reflection to remove "mistakes you shouldn't make" from your professional story, presenting a cleaner narrative.

### 2. Conviction and True Leadership
*   **Personal Story**: Shared a successful pushback at Flipkart to illustrate the power of conviction ("what is right for the business" > seniority).
*   **Leadership Definition**: Possessing a **distinct worldview**, attracting **followers**, and exhibiting **high agency** (not waiting for orders).

### 3. Core Beliefs (North Star)
*   **Definition**: Encouraged defining your own core beliefs/values.
*   **Application**: Use this inner clarity to guide major **career transition decisions** and explain the logic behind your past portfolio of bets.

### 4. Strategic Career Planning Model
*   **Framework**: Look ahead at:
    1.  **Next Role**
    2.  **Next-to-Next Role**
    3.  **Eventual Goal** (e.g., CPO, Founder)
*   **Company Classification**: Classify target companies based on current needs (e.g., maximizing learning vs. stability):
    *   **Dream**
    *   **Stepping Stone**
    *   **Paycheck**

### 5. The Interview Introduction (Tactical)
*   **Components**:
    1.  **Personal Origin Story** (Core *Why* / Childhood Influence).
    2.  **Expertise** (What you've done).
    3.  **Impact** (Goal of creating impact).
*   **Limit**: All within a **three-minute** time limit.
