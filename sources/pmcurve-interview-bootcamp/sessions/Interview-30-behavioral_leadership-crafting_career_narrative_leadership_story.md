# Crafting Your Career Narrative and Leadership Story

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** guides you on how to construct a compelling career narrative and defines the essence of effective leadership in a product context.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Career as a Narrative
*   **Definition**: A career is a **narrative** built from a **portfolio of bets** (high risk, high reward, high learning).
*   **Alignment**: These bets must align with a single **Big Goal** or **North Star** (e.g., learning, mission, financial reward).

### 2. Building the Narrative
*   **Steps**:
    1.  Explicitly list past **product experiences**.
    2.  Log **major wins**.
    3.  Identify **consistent themes** (e.g., AI/Data Science, zero-to-one products).

### 3. Effective Leadership
*   **Definition**: Leadership is defined by having **followers**.
*   **Earned By**: Possessing a **unique view of the world**, demonstrating **accountability**, and showing commitment to core beliefs.

### 4. Disagree and Commit
*   **High-Stakes Situation**: Disagreeing with superiors.
*   **Response**: The appropriate response is **Disagree and Commit**.
*   **Execution**: Show integrity by documenting all concerns ("foolproofing your stories") before executing, demonstrating maturity.

### 5. Interview Introduction (Tell Me About Yourself)
*   **Format**: **Three minutes or less**.
*   **Structure**:
    1.  **What you've done** (achievements).
    2.  **Why you've done it** (tying to your North Star/core belief).
    3.  **Where you're going** (interest in the role).

### 6. Strategic Goal
*   **Clarity**: The goal of behavioral prep is to achieve **career clarity**.
*   **Outcome**: Clarity simplifies decision-making (e.g., job choice, salary trade-offs) and leads to a **happier and more successful career**.
