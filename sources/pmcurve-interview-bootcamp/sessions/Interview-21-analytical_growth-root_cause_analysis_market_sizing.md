# Mastering Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Market Sizing

**Analytical & Growth Module**

In this video, **Deepak** covers two critical analytical interview types: Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Market Sizing/Guesstimates, providing structured frameworks for both.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Framework
*   **Process**:
    1.  **Clarify Context**: Gather initial data.
    2.  **Generate Hypotheses**: Internal vs. External factors.
    3.  **Break Down Hypotheses**: Map factors to data.
    4.  **Propose Solutions**: Short-term and Long-term.
*   **Goal**: Not just identifying the cause, but proposing **well-rounded short-term fixes and long-term policies** (e.g., implementing unit tests to prevent future bugs).

### 2. RCA Factors Checklist
*   **Internal**: Platform changes, Infrastructure, Product changes (new features/categories), Bugs.
*   **External**: Competition, Policy changes, Seasonality, User behavior shifts.

### 3. Guesstimates & Market Sizing Framework
*   **Framework**:
    1.  **Clarify**: Define product limits, unit of measure.
    2.  **Choose Approach**: Top-down (Market share) or Bottom-up (Demand/Usage).
    3.  **Calculate**: Use simple, rounded numbers.
    4.  **Sense-Check**: Validate against real-world data/insights.

### 4. Market Sizing Example: Self-Driving Cars
*   **Constraint Analysis**: Demonstrated how to size an innovative product market.
    *   *Logic*: Constrain by **affordability** and **likelihood of adoption**.
*   **Shift**: Noted how the bottleneck shifts from **demand-side** (who wants it) to **supply-side** (manufacturing/scaling) constraints for new technology.
