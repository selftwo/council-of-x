# Problem Statement #4 (Strategy): Airbnb for Business Travel

**Product Strategy Practice**

This resource covers the fourth practice problem statement in the Product Strategy module.

## Problem Statement

**As a PM at Airbnb, how would you convince leadership to get into business travel?**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your proposal.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   **Market Opportunity**: Size of business travel market vs. leisure.
    *   **Strategic Fit**: Does this leverage existing supply (listings) or require new ones?
    *   **Right to Win**: Why would business travelers choose Airbnb over hotels? (Cost, "Live like a local", longer stays).
    *   **Implementation**: What features are needed? (Receipts, "Business Ready" badge, Wi-Fi guarantees).
