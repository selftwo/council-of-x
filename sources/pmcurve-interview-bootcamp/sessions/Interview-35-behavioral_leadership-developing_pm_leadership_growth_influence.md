# Developing PM Leadership for Growth and Influence

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** discusses the core components of PM leadership and how they relate to interview success and professional growth.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Values and Beliefs
*   **Foundation**: Leadership starts with understanding one's own values.
*   **Tools**: Use tools like the **Big Five Test** and engaging in regular **reflection/journaling**.
*   **Outcome**: Builds the **conviction** necessary to make difficult decisions.

### 2. Ability to Get Things Done (Agency)
*   **High Agency**: Explores the concept of "High Agency" (internal locus of control).
*   **Mindset**: Cultivate a **growth mindset** and **psychological ownership** as a crucial habit for overcoming professional obstacles and driving results.

### 3. Communication
*   **Persuasion**: Details persuasion techniques using **Aristotle's Ethos, Pathos, and Logos** and **Cialdini's Principles of Influence**.
*   **Structure**: Emphasizes the **Minto Pyramid Principle** for structured, logical communication.

### 4. Courage
*   **Virtue**: Highlights **courage** as a vital, unfakeable leadership virtue.
*   **Impact**: Courage is necessary for driving high performance and surfacing truth in teams, which fosters **psychological safety**.
