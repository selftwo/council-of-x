# Reflecting and Strategizing Your Career Path

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** shifts the focus to long-term career strategy, encouraging you to view your journey as a portfolio and maintain high agency in your decisions.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Career as a Portfolio of Bets
*   **Concept**: View your professional journey as a **portfolio of bets** (high risk/reward/learning).
*   **Goal**: Align these bets to a singular **Big Goal** (your North Star).
*   **Narrative**: This approach clarifies patterns (e.g., preference for high learning, mission alignment) and provides a **consistent narrative** for interviewers.

### 2. Transparency, Accountability, and Conflict
*   **Conflict with Authority**: The correct response is to **voice your opinion** based on data/context and then **disagree and commit**.
*   **Trait**: This demonstrates **maturity**, trustworthiness, and accountability.

### 3. Conviction and Audacity
*   **Example**: Used a personal anecdote (decision reversal at Flipkart) to illustrate the power of conviction.
*   **Lesson**: A strong belief in **"what is right for the business"** provides the necessary confidence (audacity) to **challenge decisions** made by superiors.

### 4. Avoiding the "Hero" Narrative
*   **Advice**: Avoid seeking a false "hero" narrative.
*   **Focus**: Provide evidence of a **growth mindset** and the ability to **learn from failures**.

### 5. Strategic Career Planning Framework
*   **Criteria**: Identify key criteria for your next role (e.g., Are my skills valued? Is the industry growing? Is compensation fair?).
*   **Classification**: Use these criteria to classify target companies into:
    *   **Dream Companies**
    *   **Stepping Stones**
    *   **Paycheck Roles**
