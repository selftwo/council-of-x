# Mastering Segmentation and the Pyramid of Strategy

**Product Strategy Module**

In this video, **Deepak** explores the power of segmentation and introduces the "Pyramid of Strategy" for ensuring alignment.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. The Product Universe
*   **Categories**: Segmented the entire product universe into three broad categories:
    1.  **Content Delivery**
    2.  **Transactional/Marketplace**
    3.  **Productivity**
*   **Insight**: Noted that there are typically no more than **10 core business models** to study, simplifying the landscape.

### 2. The Pyramid of Strategy
*   **Structure**: Introduced the "Pyramid of Strategy" hierarchy:
    *   **Vision/Why**
    *   **Goals**
    *   **Business Strategy**
    *   **Product Strategy**
    *   **Roadmap**
*   **Alignment**: Stressed that every choice must **align with the highest level of abstraction** (the company's Why).

### 3. The Importance of Segmentation
*   ** Skill**: Called segmentation the **most powerful skill** for PMs.
*   **Utility**: It is critical for **narrowing down complex options** in strategy, product sense, and analytical problems.

### 4. Strategic Framework Application
*   **Case Study**: Demonstrated how to apply the framework to complex questions like **"What should Meta build in travel?"**.
*   **Process**: Quickly moving from broad context to **Positioning**, **SWOT analysis**, and finally to a **coherent product recommendation**.

### 5. Practice Approach
*   **Method**: Encouraged continuous **"slow solving"** of problems.
*   **Growth**: Raising specific personal struggles and solving a maximum number of problems helps develop a **compounding advantage**.
