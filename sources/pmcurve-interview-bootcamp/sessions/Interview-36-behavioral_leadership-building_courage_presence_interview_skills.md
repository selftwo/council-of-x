# Building Courage, Presence, and Interview Skills

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** covers the critical soft skills needed for PM leadership—courage and executive presence—and provides tactical advice for handling engineering politics and interview challenges.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Courage and Executive Presence
*   **Essential Skills**: Courage and executive presence are **critical skills**, not just traits.
*   **Application**: They are especially vital for navigating **difficult times**. Courage and presence are deeply correlated.

### 2. Dealing with Engineering Politics
*   **Challenge**: Managing political challenges with engineering leadership.
*   **First Step**: Before engaging in politics, a PM must **internalize and evaluate the value they are adding** to the team. Confidence comes from value.

### 3. Interview Preparation Strategy
*   **Inbound**: Optimize your **LinkedIn profile** to attract inbound interest.
*   **Outbound**: Focus outbound applications on the **right roles** to secure referrals and achieve higher conversion rates, rather than spraying and praying.

### 4. Handling Interview Challenges
*   **Active Listening**: Use techniques like **rephrasing questions** and taking a **brief pause** before responding to ensure understanding and composure.
*   **Thinking vs. Speaking**: Address the tendency to think and speak simultaneously by **writing down thoughts first**, then grouping and refining them before answering.
*   **Practice**: The best advice is to **practice all these techniques in your real-world job** to become a more effective PM naturally, which then translates to interviews.
