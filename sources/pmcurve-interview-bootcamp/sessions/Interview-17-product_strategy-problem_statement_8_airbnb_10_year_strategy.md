# Problem Statement #8 (Strategy): Airbnb's 10-Year Strategy

**Product Strategy Practice**

This resource covers the eighth practice problem statement in the Product Strategy module.

## Problem Statement

**You're the CEO of Airbnb - create and share your 10-year strategy.**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your strategy.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   **Vision & Mission**: Start with the "Why". Where should Airbnb be in 10 years? (Travel vs. Living vs. Connection).
    *   **Three Horizons**: Strategy for Now (Core), Next (Adjacent), and Future (Transformational).
    *   **Strategic Bets**: Specific initiatives (e.g., Experiences, Long-term stays, Business travel, Services).
    *   **Risks**: Regulatory, Competitive, Economic.
