**Product Sense Practice**

## Problem Statement

**Why do most people dislike air travel?**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your answer.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   Map out the end-to-end user journey (Booking -> Airport -> Flight -> Arrival).
    *   Identify specific pain points at each stage (e.g., security checks, cramped seating, delays, baggage claim).
    *   Consider emotional aspects (anxiety, boredom, lack of control).
    *   Brainstorm potential solutions or improvements.
