# Applying Strategy: Timing, Advantage, and Market Choice

**Product Strategy Module**

In this video, **Deepak** expands on applying strategy, moving from industry selection to timing, competitive advantage, aud market choices.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Reinforced Strategy Questions
Recapped the three initial strategy questions:
1.  **Defining the Industry**
2.  **Assessing Attractiveness** (via Porter's Five Forces)
3.  **Evaluating Existential Threat**

### 2. JBTD in New Markets
*   **Challenge**: applying Jobs to be Done (JTBD) to new markets is difficult.
*   **Goal**: The aim is to define the **context and motivation** of the job well enough to demonstrate strong analytical capability.

### 3. Right Time to Enter
*   **Critical Decision**: Explored when to enter a market.
*   **Triggers**: Major shifts create temporary openings by **creating value and destroying value** elsewhere:
    *   **Technological** shifts
    *   **Platform** shifts
    *   **Regulatory** shifts
    *   **Consumer Behavior** shifts
*   *Example*: Jio's data pricing strategy enabling the rise of social shopping.

### 4. Right to Win
*   **SWOT Analysis**: Introduced SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) to look **inwards**.
*   **Core Competencies**: Assess the company's internal strengths vs. weaknesses (e.g., Google's strength in software/data vs. weakness in logistics).

### 5. Seven Powers & Generic Strategy
*   **Seven Powers**: Presented as a checklist for defining **competitive advantage** (e.g., Cornered Resource, Network Economies).
*   **Generic Strategy**: Determining the correct approach:
    *   **Cost Leadership** vs. **Differentiation**
    *   **Broad** vs. **Niche** scope
