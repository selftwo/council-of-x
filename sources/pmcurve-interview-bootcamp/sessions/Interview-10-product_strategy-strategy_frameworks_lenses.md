# Understanding Product Strategy and Strategic Lenses

**Product Strategy Module**

In this video, **Deepak** shifts the focus to Product Strategy, defining what it is and introducing key frameworks.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Product Sense vs. Product Strategy
*   **Distinction**: Strategy involves decisions with a **longer timeframe, wider scope, and higher impact** (e.g., product line strategy) compared to product sense.
*   **Core Skills**: While strong **product sense** is the foundation, strategy interviews heavily assess **domain knowledge** and the ability to apply **strategy frameworks**.

### 2. Defining True Strategy
*   **Definition**: Strategy is not just planning; it is making an **integrated set of bold, coherent choices** that reinforce each other to position a company to **win** (referenced Roger Martin).

### 3. Why Companies Struggle
*   **The Trap**: Most companies struggle because of the **discomfort of making hard, irreversible choices**.
*   **Planning Trap**: There is often a perceived need to prove success *a priori*, leading to planning instead of strategizing.

### 4. Strategic Lenses (6Q Framework)
Introduced the **Strategic Lenses** as part of the 6Q Framework, which form the foundation of "where to play" and "how to win":
*   **Right Industry**
*   **Right Time to Enter**
*   **Right to Win**
