# Problem Statement #2 (Analytical): LinkedIn Jobs Metrics

**Analytical Practice**

This resource covers the second practice problem statement in the Analytical & Growth module, focusing on Success Metrics for a marketplace.

## Problem Statement

**Define goals and success metrics of LinkedIn jobs marketplace.**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your response.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   **Marketplace Dynamics**: Identify the two sides (Job Seekers vs. Recruiters/Companies).
    *   **Goals**: What does success look like for Seekers (Finding a job)? For Recruiters (Finding a candidate)? For LinkedIn (Liquidity, Revenue)?
    *   **Metrics**:
        *   **North Star**: e.g., Confirmed Hires (Ideal but hard to track) vs. Applications per Job.
        *   **Seeker Metrics**: Application Rate, Response Rate.
        *   **Recruiter Metrics**: Quality Candidates per Post, Time to Hire.
    *   **Trade-offs**: Quality vs. Quantity of applications.
