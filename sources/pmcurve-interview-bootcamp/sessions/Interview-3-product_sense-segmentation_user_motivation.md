# Deep Dive: Segmentation and Understanding User Motivation

**Session 2.3**

In this video, **Deepak** explores the complexities of segmentation in PM interviews, emphasizing psychographics and user empathy.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. The Challenge of Segmentation
*   Stated that **segmentation is the most difficult part** of PM interviews.
*   **Definition**: A segment is defined as a group that **needs a fundamentally different product or solution**.

### 2. Psychographic Segmentation
*   **Focus**: Stressed the importance of **psychographic segmentation** over just demographic or behavioral data.
*   **Core Question**: Focus on **why people do what they do**.

### 3. Empathy and the NDC Framework
*   **Cognitive vs. Affective**: Introduced **cognitive empathy** (understanding their perspective) as superior to affective empathy (feeling what they feel) in this context.
*   **NDC Framework**: Use **Needs, Desires, and Compulsions** to build this empathy.

### 4. Job-To-Be-Done (JTBD)
*   **Definition**: What people are "hiring" your product to do.
*   **Analysis**: Analyze JTBD through three aspects:
    *   **Functional**
    *   **Emotional**
    *   **Social**

### 5. Segment Prioritization
*   **Method**: Explain segment prioritization by cross-referencing dimensions with criteria.
*   **Dimensions**: e.g., **Goals** and **Digital Fluency**.
*   **Criteria**:
    *   **Size**
    *   **Severity**
    *   **Willingness to Pay**
