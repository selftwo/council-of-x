The trick in the Fermi’s estimate is to estimate values using only orders of magnitude,

a.k.a. powers of ten. For example, if we have to place a bet on the number of cars in

India per person, we have to choose in the orders of magnitude, i.e. between 0.1, 1, or

10, 100..etc.
