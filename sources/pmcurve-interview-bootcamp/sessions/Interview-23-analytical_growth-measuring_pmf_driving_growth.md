# Measuring Product-Market Fit and Driving Growth

**Analytical & Growth Module**

In this video, **Deepak** discusses the nuances of measuring Product-Market Fit (PMF) and introduces frameworks for driving sustainable growth.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Product-Market Fit (PMF) Dynamics
*   **Nature**: Value is relative and PMF is **always changing**.
*   **Example**: PMF erosion in education due to LLMs requires constant re-evaluation.

### 2. Measuring PMF
*   **High-Frequency Products**: Track **retention curves**. Flattening at a non-zero value indicates strong PMF.
*   **Infrequent Products**: Use metrics like **CSAT** (Customer Satisfaction) and **referrals**.

### 3. Retention & Growth
*   **Relationship**: Higher long-term retention leads to continuous MAU growth.
*   **Warning**: Zero retention means growth is unsustainable and only fueled by spending.
*   **Components**: Segment (Who?), Activation (Aha moment?), Engagement (Deep usage?).

### 4. GTM Framework
Introduced the foundational **Go-to-Market (GTM) Framework** for acquisition:
*   **Product**
*   **Market**
*   **Model** (Pricing)
*   **Channel** (Paid vs. Organic)

### 5. Channel-Model Fit & Strategy
*   **Channel-Model Fit**: Explained the **"dead zone"** where pricing is too high for self-serve but too low for sales, making growth unsustainable.
*   **Lever Selection**: Effective growth strategy requires making conscious choices about which levers (**Channel, Product, Market**) to pull.
