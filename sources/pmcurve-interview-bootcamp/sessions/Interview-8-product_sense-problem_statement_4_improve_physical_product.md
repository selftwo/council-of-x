# Problem Statement #4: Improve a Physical Product

**Product Sense Practice**

This resource covers the fourth practice problem statement in the Product Sense module.

## Problem Statement

**Take a physical product, and improve it significantly.**

## Instructions

*   **Attempt the problem yourself first**: Before watching the solution video, spend some time structuring your answer.
*   **Focus Areas**:
    *   Choose a common physical product (e.g., umbrella, chair, alarm clock).
    *   Identify current pain points or limitations.
    *   Propose solution improvements (incremental vs. radical).
    *   Define metrics for "significant" improvement.
