# Managing Design and Engineering Challenges as PM

**Behavioral & Leadership Module**

In this video, **Deepak** discusses how to handle major challenges in PM interviews, focusing on cross-functional collaboration with design and engineering teams.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Design Challenges
*   **Issues**: Design teams getting too creative or "scope creep."
*   **Solutions**:
    *   **Regular Check-ins**: Ensure alignment.
    *   **PM Mockups**: PMs creating **crude wireframes/mockups** to refine PRDs and maintain focus on product goals.

### 2. Engineering Challenges
*   **Disagreement**: Disagreements on feature building.
*   **Solution**: Tie features back to **shared company/product goals** and provide **evidence** to support proposals. Avoid opinion-based arguments.

### 3. Execution & Estimation
*   **Overestimation**: If engineers overestimate effort, trust the **Engineering Manager (EM)**.
*   **Over-engineering**: Recommend **MVPs/POCs** (Proof of Concepts) and strong processes to validate before building extensively.

### 4. Conviction & Failure
*   **Mindset**: Importance of a PM's **conviction** in their vision.
*   **Resilience**: Accepting **failure and experimentation** as a natural part of the job.
