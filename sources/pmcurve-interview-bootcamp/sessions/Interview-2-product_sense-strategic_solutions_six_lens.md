# Advanced Product Sense: Strategic Solutions and the Six-Lens Framework

**Session 2.2**

In this video, **Deepak** dives into advanced strategies for Product Sense interviews, distinguishing between junior and senior candidate responses and introducing the "Six Lens" framework.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Seniority in Solutions
*   **Comparison**: Solutions are compared based on seniority.
*   **Expectation**: Senior candidates (GPM/VP) must offer answers that a company would **actually want to implement**.
*   It's not enough to be just factually correct or provide basic solutions; the response must demonstrate **implementable strategic value** (e.g., integrating AI voice).

### 2. Strategic Thinking & Creativity
*   **Hypothesis over Interrogation**: Emphasized that strategic thinking and creativity are crucial for senior roles.
*   Candidates should **make and validate intelligent hypotheses** rather than behaving like a consultant and asking numerous clarifying questions.

### 3. Clarifying Vague Prompts
Detailed the **four key areas** to focus on when clarifying a vague product prompt:
1.  **Underlying Goal**
2.  **Who** are we solving for?
3.  **Scope and Boundaries**
4.  **Constraints/Limitations**

### 4. The "Six Lens" Framework
Introduced the **"Six Lens" framework** for structuring comprehensive solutions. This framework covers two main aspects:

**Strategic Aspects:**
*   **Industry**
*   **Time to Enter**
*   **Right to Win**

**Product Sense Aspects:**
*   **Segment**
*   **Product**
*   **Positioning**
