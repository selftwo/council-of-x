## Structure

Four modules of numbered session notes in `sessions/`. Each note is a compact framework summary (30 to 40 lines); seven sessions also have fuller `-transcript.md` companions (~200 lines).

- **Module 1, Product Sense (1 to 9):** PSHE scoping, Six-Lens solutions, NDC/JTBD segmentation, CRiCE/MECE prioritization, then five practice problems.
- **Module 2, Product Strategy (10 to 18):** 6Q framework, Porter, 7 Powers, timing and Right to Win, Pyramid of Strategy, then four case problems (Google food, Meta travel, Airbnb 10-year, Airbnb business travel).
- **Module 3, Analytical & Growth (19 to 27):** north star metrics and problem types, actor-based metric breakdown, RCA and market sizing, C.H.A.S.A., PMF measurement and retention curves, growth RCA (DAU/MAU decomposition), pricing tradeoffs (Waymo case), then two metric problems. This module overlaps most with `sources/pmcurve-advanced-growth/`.
- **Module 4, Behavioral & Leadership (28 to 36):** STAR plus learnings, career narrative, conviction, career innings, Minto Pyramid and Cialdini, executive presence and courage. Sessions 28 to 36 are close to onthespot's territory: speaking under pressure, storytelling, presence.

`course-transcripts/` holds the live cohort sessions (raw speech-to-text, unedited): resumes and the 4 C's (01), course philosophy (02), market sizing (03), analytical interviews (04), execution and GTM (05), course retro (06), live Blinkit product sense exercise (07), 90 to 150 second self-intro craft (08), data/AI products stub (09), plus 10 and an unnumbered behavioral session on STAR vs narrative arc.
